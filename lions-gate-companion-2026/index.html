A Practice To Return To

The 8/8 Ritual, Repeat It Anytime

Light a candle, gold or white if you have it, whatever you have if you don't. Before you light it, write down one thing you're ready to release and one thing you're ready to call in, just a sentence each, nothing has to sound profound.

Light the candle on both. Sit for five to ten minutes. Let whatever comes up move through you without needing to fix or explain it, that's the whole practice, presence, not resolution.

When you're ready, blow out the candle and say, out loud if you can, "and so it is."
