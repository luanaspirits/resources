The Teaching

Three Mirroring Myths, Flipped

Tap a card to see the reframe shared on the call.

Myth 01 "Everything he does is a mirror of my wounds." Tap to reveal →
Reframe Sometimes it's simply a trigger, a boundary moment, or plain unworkable behavior. Not every mirror is a direct mirror.
Myth 02 "If I heal enough, he'll finally change." Tap to reveal →
Reframe You cannot heal your way into someone else's maturity. His growth is his to do, not a prize you unlock for him.
Myth 03 "The masculine is the mirror." Tap to reveal →
Reframe He's part of the mirror, not the whole mirror. The universe as a whole reflects your beliefs; he is only one piece of it.

Here's the piece most twin flame teachings skip: mirroring isn't just one thing. On the call, we named a fourth kind, the contrast mirror, sometimes called "Reverse UNO." That's when your open, regulated energy doesn't reflect his wound back to him gently, it makes the gap between where he is and where you are impossible for him to ignore. Direct mirror, trigger, belief mirror, contrast mirror, each one asks something different of you, and lumping them together is exactly how this teaching gets weaponized against your worth.
